### 1. LEAD — Iran vows to defy the US blockade (Gulf / Middle East)

American naval blockade, a day after President Trump threatened to sink
Iranian ships trying to break it. Iranian state media also reported that
Iranian air defences shot down a US drone east of the Strait of Hormuz.
This is the day's most consequential story: a direct military standoff
around the world's busiest oil chokepoint, with tankers already reported

NATO will deploy an AWACS early-warning aircraft to Poland after three
Russian Kh-101 missiles landed in the country on the alliance's eastern
flank. The deployment is the alliance's direct response to a spillover
incident on NATO territory.

Source: Reuters world news summary, 24 Nov 2026.

### 4. Huang returns to Nvidia; shares soar 31% (United States / markets)

Eleven days after resigning as chief executive, Jensen Huang was reinstated
at Nvidia after President Trump assured the board he posed no national
security threat. Nvidia shares rose more than 30%, lifting the Nasdaq 6.5%
and the S&P 500 4.7% in what Reuters called one of the most extraordinary
days in US market history.

Source: Reuters "Trading Day", 23 Nov 2026.

Source: Reuters world news summary, 24 Nov 2026.

  day's most extraordinary single event.
- **Faithfulness:** every figure on the page comes from the reports cited
  above — "vows", "says" and "confirmed" are kept as reported claims, not
